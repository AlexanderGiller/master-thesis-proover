\section{Task and Requirements}
\label{sec:requirements}

This chapter states the task of \TSTPy{}, derives the verdict policy from the scoring scheme and summarizes the requirements that the design has to meet.

\paragraph{Task and assumptions.}
\label{subsec:req-context}

The verifier receives a FOF problem file and a TSTP proof file and decides whether the proof is a valid refutation of the negated conjecture in the sense of Section~\ref{subsec:verifier-def}.
The proofs use the roles \texttt{axiom}, \texttt{conjecture}, \texttt{negated\_conjecture} and \texttt{plain}, and the statuses \texttt{thm}, \texttt{esa} and \texttt{cth}.
The rules fix the essential requirements and deliberately leave the handling of edge cases to the participants as part of the task.
It is assumed that the problem file is trusted and the proof file is not, that proofs can be large and deeply nested and that an external ATP may or may not be available.
Where the rules leave room for interpretation, the verifier follows the conservative reading and never accepts without evidence.

\paragraph{Scoring and the verdict policy.}
\label{subsec:req-scoring}

Under the ProoVer 2026 scheme a wrong acceptance costs $-10$, whereas a correct acceptance only gains $+1$, a correct rejection $+2$, a wrong rejection $-1$ and an abstention $0$ (Section~\ref{subsec:proover2026}).
A single wrong acceptance therefore cancels ten correct ones, while an abstention costs at most the points of one proof.
The verifier has no way to estimate how likely an undecided step is to be valid. It therefore does not guess.
It accepts a proof only if every step has been validated, rejects it only if it has found a concrete defect and abstains in all other cases, for example if no checker exists for a rule or the prover gives no answer.
This \emph{fail-closed}, three-valued policy is the central decision of the thesis. All other requirements follow from it or from the competition setting.

\paragraph{Requirements.}
\label{subsec:req-list}

\begin{description}
  \item[Soundness.] A proof is accepted only if every step has been validated. Missing tools, tool errors and unparsable output never lead to acceptance. A step with an unknown rule name is accepted only if the external prover confirms that it follows from its premises.
  \item[Coverage.] The verifier parses FOF problems and TSTP proofs with all annotations, checks the import of axioms and conjectures up to alpha-equivalence, the negated conjecture, the dependency graph, the final \texttt{\$false}, and Skolemization internally, and checks the other rules of the benchmark internally where this is feasible.
  \item[Restricted delegation.] Steps without an internal checker are passed to an external ATP only if their status is \texttt{thm} or \texttt{cth}, and only together with their own premises.
  \item[Resources.] Every proof is processed within the wall-clock limit of 30\,s, and malformed or deeply nested input does not crash the verifier.
  \item[Diagnostics and extensibility.] Every finding names the step and a reason, and a rule can be added or tested without changing the checkers of the other rules.
\end{description}
